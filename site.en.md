# About me

I am a data engineer with over 18 years of experience in IT and the owner of **Legnumcloud sp. z o.o.** I have worked for companies in the automotive, pharmaceutical, telecommunications and gas industries. I am passionate about Business Intelligence and, more recently, about building software with the help of AI.

### Good collaboration starts with a conversation

Before I get to the technology, I want to understand what you want to achieve and what problem we are solving. **I focus on careful listening, the right questions and plain language** — so that we understand each other even if you don't work in IT. I value open communication and looking for solutions together.

I combine technical experience with a startup founder's perspective. Previously as **CEO of Trackick**, and today as **co-founder of [roboprocess](https://www.roboprocess.pl)**, I also look at software from the product and business side: who it should help, what is really needed and where it makes sense to start.

I am open-minded. I am interested not only in how systems work, but also in how people think and make decisions — which is one of the reasons I am interested in psychology. I want to be a partner you can think an idea through with, talk to without technical jargon, and move on to a concrete solution.
